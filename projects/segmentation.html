Adoption Risk Segmentation

A cohort risk model that turned renewal from a surprise into a signal — flagging at-risk ARR early enough to intervene, and lifting renewal rates through targeted plays instead of blanket outreach.

The situation

Renewals were being lost the way most renewals are lost — quietly, and then all at once. There was no early-warning system: churn showed up in the renewal report, which is exactly too late to do anything about it. Success capacity was spread evenly across the base instead of aimed where risk actually lived.

The model

Every account was scored on three signals — usage trend, engagement depth, and renewal proximity — then sorted into a risk grid. Where an account lands dictates the play: intervene, watch, or grow.

The plays

  • At risk — declining usage inside 90 days of renewal: exec sponsor, value review, and a save plan triggered automatically.
  • Watch — flat usage or single-threaded: adoption nudges and multi-threading before the tier slips.
  • Grow — healthy and expanding: routed to expansion motions instead of retention effort.

The outcome

  • ~$12M of at-risk ARR surfaced early enough to act on.
  • Renewal rates up ~18% in the covered cohort via targeted intervention.
  • Success capacity reallocated from blanket check-ins to the accounts that actually needed them.

Churn stopped being a quarterly autopsy and became a weekly, workable signal.
