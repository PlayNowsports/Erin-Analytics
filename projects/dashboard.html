The situation

Every leadership meeting started with a data argument. Numbers came from three spreadsheets, each a few weeks stale, each maintained by a different person. By the time the "truth" was assembled, it was already out of date — and no two dashboards agreed.

The build

A single executive view wired directly to CRM, refreshing on its own. Headline KPIs up top, trend and territory detail below — designed so an exec gets the state of the business in one glance and can drill without a data request.

What changed

  • Meetings started with decisions, not data reconciliation.
  • Coverage and win-rate erosion became visible in-week, not at quarter-end.
  • Territory health surfaced where reps needed air cover before the number slipped.
  • Spreadsheet maintenance — and the errors that came with it — went to zero.

The dashboard didn't just report the business. It set the operating cadence the whole team ran on.
