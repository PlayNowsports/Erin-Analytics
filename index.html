02 — Approach

Opinion in. System out.

Every engagement follows the same rhythm — diagnose what's true, model what's possible, wire it into the operation, then hand you the keys.

Diagnose

Audit the data, the pipeline, and the assumptions. Find where the forecast breaks and why territories are misaligned.

Model

Build the TAM, the segmentation, and the forecast — grounded in real conversion and retention math, not gut feel.

Operationalize

Wire it into CRM, dashboards, and a weekly cadence so the system runs without the consultant in the room.

Scale

Simulate the next move — headcount, quota, pricing — so growth decisions are tested before they're funded.
